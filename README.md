# Noyob Innovative website

A fast, SEO-ready static website. No build tools are needed: upload the whole folder to any host
(Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Hostinger or cPanel hosting).

## Before you publish
1. **Domain**: search and replace `https://www.your-domain.com` with your real domain in all `.html` files, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.
2. **Contact details and forms**: open `assets/config.js` and fill in email, phone, WhatsApp, booking link and social links.
   Empty items stay hidden. For forms, create a free Formspree form and paste its URL into `formEndpoint`.
   If you only set `email`, the forms open the visitor's email app instead.
3. **Sample case studies**: the three projects (retail POS, UAE car rental AI scanning, grocery delivery app) are SAMPLES with
   placeholder text, figures and quotes. They are marked "noindex" and left out of `sitemap.xml` so search engines ignore them.
   Replace the content with real, client-approved projects, then remove the sample badges and the noindex tag.
4. **Images**: the images in `assets/img` are brand-style illustrations. Replace them with real project screenshots and team photos
   when you have them, keeping descriptive alt text.
5. **Review the drafts**: service descriptions, FAQ answers, timelines, the tools list, city names and the privacy policy are drafts.
   Check they match how Noyob Innovative actually works. Have the privacy policy reviewed before launch.
6. **Search**: after launch, submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and create your Google Business Profile.

## Pages
index, services, 5 service pages, work, 3 sample case studies, about, careers, insights (4 articles), contact, privacy, 404.
