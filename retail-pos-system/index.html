Cloud POS system for a multi-branch retail chain

A growing supermarket group needed one billing system for all its branches. We designed and built a cloud POS that keeps every counter fast, every branch in sync on stock and every owner clear on sales.

Client
Sample retail group
Industry
Retail and supermarkets
Timeline
14 weeks
Services
UI/UX design, Web app development, Data analytics
Platform
Web, tablet and desktop

The challenge

What was getting in the way.

The group ran separate billing software in each branch. Stock counts were updated by hand at the end of the day, so popular items ran out without warning while others sat unsold.

Cashiers were slowed down by crowded screens, and a weak internet connection in two branches meant bills could not be completed at peak hours. Management waited until the next morning for a consolidated sales report.

Our solution

How we solved it.

We started with a two-week discovery phase, shadowing cashiers and store managers to see where time was lost. From that we designed a simplified billing screen with large touch targets, barcode scanning and quick keys for the most common items.

The POS was built as a progressive web app with an offline mode that stores bills locally and syncs them when the connection returns. A central inventory service updates stock across branches in real time, and a management dashboard shows sales, stock and staff performance as they happen.

    What we built

    Key features.

    • Fast billing

      Barcode scanning, quick keys and split payments by cash, card, UPI or wallet.

    • Offline mode

      Bills continue during internet outages and sync automatically afterwards.

    • Live inventory

      Stock updates instantly across branches, with low-stock alerts and transfers between stores.

    • GST invoices and receipts

      Tax-ready invoices, thermal receipt printing and digital receipts by WhatsApp or email.

    • Roles and permissions

      Separate access for cashiers, store managers and owners, with a full audit trail.

    • Sales dashboard

      Real-time sales by branch, hour and product, with daily summaries sent to management.

    Our process

    How we built it.

    1. Discover

      Two weeks of store visits, interviews with cashiers and a review of existing reports.

    2. Design

      Wireframes and a clickable prototype tested with cashiers before development.

    3. Build

      Six sprints with a demo after each one, starting with billing and ending with reporting.

    4. Launch

      A pilot in one branch, staff training, then a phased rollout to all branches.
