AI vehicle inspection app for a car rental company in the UAE

A UAE car rental company was losing time and money on damage disputes at vehicle handover. We built an app that guides staff through a photo scan and uses AI to detect and document damage in minutes.

Client
Sample UAE car rental company
Industry
Car rental and mobility
Timeline
5 months
Services
App development, AI integration, UI/UX design, Cloud and DevOps
Platform
iOS, Android and admin web panel

The challenge

What was getting in the way.

Every handover relied on a paper checklist and a handful of phone photos. Small scratches were easy to miss, and when a car came back with damage it was hard to prove whether it had been there before.

Inspections took around twenty minutes per car at busy branches, and disputes with customers took days to resolve. The company also needed to serve English and Arabic speaking staff and customers.

Our solution

How we solved it.

We designed a guided capture flow that tells the agent exactly where to stand and which angle to photograph, using on-screen frames and live feedback. Six standard angles are captured for every handover, plus optional close-ups.

A computer vision model compares check-out and check-in photos, highlights new scratches and dents, estimates severity and produces a PDF condition report with timestamps. Staff review the findings, add notes, and the customer signs digitally. The app and the report are available in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layouts.

What we built

Key features.

  • Guided 360 photo capture

    On-screen frames and live prompts make sure every angle is captured clearly.

  • AI damage detection

    Scratches and dents are detected, outlined and graded by severity.

  • Check-out and check-in comparison

    Photos are compared automatically so new damage is easy to prove.

  • Document scanning

    Driving licence and ID details are read with OCR to speed up the handover.

  • English and Arabic

    Full right-to-left support for staff and customer-facing screens.

  • Admin dashboard

    Fleet status, returns, open disputes and downloadable reports in one place.

Our process

How we built it.

  1. Discover

    Workshops with branch staff and a review of past damage disputes to define what the AI needed to detect.

  2. Design

    Capture flow prototypes tested in a real branch car park, in daylight and low light.

  3. Build

    App, backend and AI pipeline built in parallel, with model accuracy reviewed every sprint.

  4. Launch

    A pilot at one branch, staff training in two languages, then rollout across the fleet.
