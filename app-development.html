Questions

Questions about app development.

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply with details.

Should I build for Android, iOS or both?

If your audience uses both, a cross-platform approach with Flutter or React Native lets us ship both from one codebase. We recommend the right approach after understanding your users and budget.

How long does app development take?

A focused first version usually takes two to four months. Apps with several user roles or integrations take longer. You get a milestone plan before development starts.

Do you help publish the app on the Play Store and App Store?

Yes. We prepare listings, screenshots and builds, handle submissions, and support you through the review process.

Can you add features to an app I already have?

Yes. We can review your current app, work with its codebase, and add features or improve performance.
