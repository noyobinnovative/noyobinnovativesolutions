What you get

What our data analytics service includes.

The goal of analytics is simple: fewer guesses and better decisions. We bring your numbers together, agree what matters and present it in dashboards your whole team can understand.

  • Business dashboards

    Live views of sales, leads, operations and finance in Power BI or Looker Studio.

  • Website and marketing analytics

    Google Analytics 4, conversion tracking and campaign attribution set up correctly.

  • Reporting automation

    Replace manual spreadsheets with reports that update on their own.

  • Data cleaning and integration

    Combine data from spreadsheets, CRMs, ad platforms and databases into one reliable source.

  • Customer and sales analysis

    Find your best customers, products and channels.

  • Trends and forecasting

    Spot patterns and plan stock, staffing and budgets with more confidence.

Questions

Questions about data analytics.

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply with details.

What data do I need to get started?

Start with what you have: sales records, website traffic, ad accounts or customer lists. We review your sources and tell you what is usable and what needs to be set up.

Is my data kept secure?

We use access controls, work only with the data a project needs, and agree on confidentiality terms before we begin.

Which dashboard tool do you use?

We usually use Power BI or Looker Studio, depending on your existing tools and budget.

Can you set up Google Analytics 4 for my website?

Yes. We configure GA4, Tag Manager and conversion events so you can see which pages and campaigns bring enquiries.
