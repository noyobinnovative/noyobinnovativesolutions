Privacy policy for Noyob Innovative

How Noyob Innovative collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated 19 October 2026.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this website, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, company, project description and, for career applications, a link to your portfolio or profile. We may also collect basic usage information, such as pages visited and device type, through analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry or application.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you request.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us run the website, such as form handling, email and analytics providers, and only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry and application details only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, and we take reasonable steps to protect them from unauthorised access.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
