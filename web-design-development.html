Questions

Questions about web design and development.

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply with details.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes three to six weeks from approved design to launch. Larger sites, online stores and custom web applications take longer. We confirm a milestone-based timeline in your proposal.

How much does a website cost?

Cost depends on the number of pages, features and integrations. After a free consultation we send an itemised estimate so you know exactly what is included before you commit.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. We build with clean semantic code, fast load times, mobile-first layouts, meta tags, structured data and a sitemap, and we can continue with ongoing SEO through our digital marketing team.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review your current site, keep what works, fix what does not, and plan redirects so you do not lose existing search rankings.
