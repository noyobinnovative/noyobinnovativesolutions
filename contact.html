Questions

Before you get in touch.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How quickly will you reply?

We aim to reply to every enquiry within one working day with next steps or a few clarifying questions.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation is free and carries no obligation. We use it to understand your goals and tell you honestly whether and how we can help.

Can you sign an NDA before I share my idea?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share confidential details about your product or business.

Can we meet in person or only online?

Both are possible. We meet clients in person in Kerala where it makes sense and work with clients across India and abroad through video calls.

What should I include in my enquiry?

A short description of your business, what you want to build or improve, any deadline and a rough budget range if you have one. If you are unsure about any of it, we can work it out together.
