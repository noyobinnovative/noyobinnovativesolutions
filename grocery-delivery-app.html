E-commerce and delivery app for a local grocery brand

A neighbourhood grocery brand with loyal local customers wanted to take orders online and deliver them the same day. We designed and built the customer app, the delivery partner app and the store admin panel.

Client
Sample grocery brand
Industry
Grocery and e-commerce
Timeline
4 months
Services
UI/UX design, App development, Admin panel, Digital marketing
Platform
iOS, Android and web admin

The challenge

What was getting in the way.

Orders arrived by phone and WhatsApp, and staff wrote them down by hand. Mistakes were common, customers could not see when an order would arrive, and the team could not handle more orders at peak times.

Delivery partners had no shared view of routes or order status, so the store spent much of the day answering where-is-my-order calls.

Our solution

How we solved it.

We designed a simple shopping flow around the customer's weekly basket: reorder in two taps, choose a delivery slot, pay by UPI or card and follow the order on a live map. Fresh items show real-time availability so customers rarely meet out-of-stock surprises.

Store staff receive orders on an admin panel with a clear order board, while delivery partners use a lightweight app with route guidance and status updates. After launch we set up SEO, local listings and a launch campaign so the app reached customers in the delivery area.

What we built

Key features.

  • Fast shopping and reorder

    Search, categories and one-tap reorder of previous baskets.

  • Delivery slots

    Customers pick a delivery window that suits them, and the store controls capacity.

  • Live order tracking

    A map shows the delivery partner and a live arrival time.

  • Secure payments

    UPI, cards and cash on delivery with instant confirmation.

  • Store admin panel

    An order board, stock updates, offers and sales reports.

  • Delivery partner app

    Route guidance, order status updates and proof of delivery.

Our process

How we built it.

  1. Discover

    Interviews with customers and store staff to map the weekly shopping journey.

  2. Design

    Mobile prototypes tested with real customers before development.

  3. Build

    Customer app, partner app and admin panel developed in parallel sprints.

  4. Launch

    A soft launch in one neighbourhood, then a marketing campaign across the delivery area.
